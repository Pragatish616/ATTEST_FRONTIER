01 — The gap

Two failures survive every grounding checker we could find.

Faithfulness metrics score the answer against the chunk. Both of these pass that test and are still wrong.

01
FAILURE 01

The checker isn't reading.

Ask a verifier whether a claim is supported by a chunk and it says yes. Ask whether the negation is supported by the same chunk — and it says yes again. The verdict was never a function of the evidence.

FRAGILE
FAILURE 02

The source itself is stale.

The answer is faithfully grounded in the retrieved chunk. The chunk was written eighteen months ago and is now false. Every faithfulness metric scores this as a pass — because faithfulness is measured against the chunk, and nobody checks the chunk.

STALE
02 — The method

Three verifiers. No shared context.

No verifier ever sees another's output. That independence is the entire design — a majority vote between three correlated judges tells you nothing.

02
V — 01

Entailment

"Does the retrieved chunk
entail this claim?"

Judged strictly against the supplied context. Evidence returns as character spans into the chunk, never quoted text — spans are checkable, quotes hallucinate.

Ordinary hallucination
V — 02

Adversarial prober

"Does the verdict survive
mutation of the claim?"

Generates negations, entity swaps and quantifier shifts, then re-verifies each through the same judge. A negated claim still marked grounded is a judge that pattern-matched instead of reading.

Catches FRAGILE
V — 03

Independent retrieval

"Does the open web
still agree?"

Re-searches the claim from scratch and cross-checks the source rather than the answer. The only verifier permitted to know the entailment verdict, so it can separate outdated from contradicted.

Catches STALE
03 — The taxonomy

Six verdicts. Two you won't find elsewhere.

03
GROUNDED
Entailed by the retrieved context, and the verdict held under every probe.
FRAGILE OURS
Entailed — but a probe failed to flip the verdict when logic says it must.
STALE OURS
Grounded in the chunk, but independent retrieval disagrees. The source went out of date.
UNSUPPORTED
The context neither supports nor contradicts the claim.
CONTRADICTED
The retrieved context directly says the opposite.
UNVERIFIABLE
Subjective, predictive, or not checkable. Precision over coverage, by design.

Reconciliation runs CONTRADICTED > STALE > UNSUPPORTED > FRAGILE > GROUNDED > UNVERIFIABLE. The highest-precedence verdict wins however many verifiers dissent — one CONTRADICTED outranks two GROUNDEDs, deliberately. Precision over consensus.
